# The reclaim zone: a sudden drop, a close above the strong high, the first tap of the untouched bottom (Nov 2026)

Generated by `node research/reclaim/reclaim_lab.js report` on 2026-11-25. Research only: the app and the bot don't use it.
Data: Binance spot, every 3m candle from each coin's listing to 25 Nov 2026 00:00 UTC; 15m, 1h, 4h and 1d are built from them, so every timeframe of a coin covers the same history.

## The rules

The base rules were fixed from the trading logic before any result was seen (README.md has the reasoning). Everything is counted in the chart's own candles and its own ATR (Wilder, 14):
- **Strong high**: the highest high of the 50 candles before it.
- **Sudden drop**: within 12 candles the low is ≥ 5 ATR under the high (the ATR of the candle before the high), and it trades under the lowest low of those 50 candles (the range's stops and liquidations).
- **Reclaim**: a candle closes above the strong high within 36 candles of the low.
- **Zone**: the bottom third of the drop, untouched between leaving it and the reclaim.
- **Trade**: a limit buy at the top of the zone on its first touch (within 500 candles of the reclaim); stop 0.1 ATR under the drop's low; target the highest high since the reclaim; out after 100 candles.
- **Fills**: a limit buy fills at its price (or a lower open); on the fill candle only the stop can fill; stops fill at the stop or a lower open.
- **Costs**: 0.1% a side, plus 0.05% (BTC, ETH, SOL) or 0.15% (the rest) slippage on stops and timed exits.
- **Control**: each trade's exact order (depth under the price, stop and target, all in ATR) placed at 50 random times in the same half of the same chart. *p* is the share of random sets (one copy per trade) that did as well.

**The choice** (the first half of each coin's history only). Each change from the base was kept if it raised the first-half average trade on a majority of the timeframes with ≥ 10 first-half trades. Only 3m and 15m had that many. Kept: **K6, M20, Rx6, f50, T300, mitig**. The chosen rules:
the drop within 6 candles; the strong high is the highest of 20 candles; the reclaim within 36 candles; the zone is the bottom HALF of the drop; out after 300 candles; the zone may have been revisited before the reclaim.
