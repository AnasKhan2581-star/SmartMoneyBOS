**Question (user, Nov 2026):** on 15m (Chart, Compare, e.g. "top 50 gainers, last 3 months", or Alerts) should Liquidity Breakout still need BTC above its 50-day MA? Is it logical there, and what would be?

**Method.**
- Data: the user's 103-coin Compare list (`CMP_DEFAULT`), full Binance 15m history for every coin (BTC and ETH from 2017-08-17), up to 2026-11-05 16:00 UTC. 103 coins ran; skipped: none.
- Rules: the shipped ones. Signals = first close over the 2-day high + over the 5-day SMA + over the 149-bar MA + volume ≥ 1.3× its 1-day average + clearing the high by ≥ 1×ATR. Exits = 4×ATR stop, close under the prior 1-day low, half at +3R.
- Each gate changes ONE thing: which candles a buy may be taken on. The replica that runs them reproduces detector.js trade for trade on every coin (`lab15.js parity`), for both the shipped gate and no gate.
- Stats are the Compare tab's own `pathStats` (read out of index.html): all-in per trade, fills at the signal close, 0.1% fee a side. "After slippage" adds 0.05% a side.
- Pooled rows put every closed trade of every coin together. "PF w/o top 2%" drops the best 2% of trades: an edge that lives only in a handful of moonshots shows up there.
- Two-year blocks are separate markets: 2018–20 (2018 bear, 2020 crash), 2021–22 (bull, then the 2022 bear), 2023–24 (recovery, bull), 2025–26 (to date).
- The basket puts equal money in every coin listed ≥ 31 days before the block (no rebalancing): it is what running the rule on the whole list would have done, drawdown included.

## Verdict

1. **Yes, keep the 50-day BTC gate on 15m. It is logical there, and it is the biggest single improvement 15m has.** With no gate, 15m Liquidity Breakout is break-even: 16,265 trades, PF 1.00, +0.01% a trade, median coin −17.6% at a −66% median drawdown. The trades the gate blocks average −0.42%, against +0.24% for the ones it keeps; chance gives a gap that large < 0.02% of the time, and kept beat blocked on 75 of 99 coins. With the gate: PF 1.09, +0.22% a trade, median coin +3.6%, more money than no gate on 72 coins (less on 31).
2. **Why it is logical even though a 15m trade lasts about a day:** the 50-day MA is not a timing signal, it is the market's regime, and a breakout's odds depend on the regime at any horizon. It earns its keep in the markets where BTC is weak: 2021–22 basket −6.3% → +21.6% (max DD −33% → −13%), 2025–26 +3.7% → +14.9%. In the 2023–24 bull it changes little, and in 2018–20 (thin data: 3 coins in that basket) it cost money. A placebo, the same gate fed BTC's state from another year, does not reproduce the gain overall or in either weak-BTC block (section 5), and a label shuffle that removes the same number of trades never matches it (section 2b): it is BTC's state, not just trading less.
3. **The faster BTC reads alone are NOT better** (BTC over its 5-day MA, its 149-bar MA, or up on the day): they let the bear markets through. On its own, "BTC over its 149-bar MA" still lets through trades that average −0.07% in 2021–22. Loosening the gate ("either": a BTC bounce opens it) is worse than the shipped gate in every block.
4. **The one 15m change that tested well: ALSO require BTC above its own 149-bar MA** (BTC must pass the same 149 MA the coin must pass, ≈37h on 15m) — `both`. It blocks another 11% of the trades the gate keeps, and those were worse in all four blocks (gap +0.66% a trade; chance 0.2% on the mean, < 0.02% on the trimmed mean). Every short MA from 1 to 5 days improves the whole-history result (a plateau); the 1-day and 149-bar versions improve every block, the 2–5-day ones lose a little in 2021–22. Result: +0.29% a trade (shipped +0.22%), PF 1.12, median coin +5.5%, more money than the shipped gate on 64 coins and less on 34 (+5.8% per coin, +6.8% after slippage). Coin by coin the evidence is thinner: the trades it blocks were worse than the ones it keeps on 43 of 73 coins. The gain is real but small: weigh it as a refinement, not a fix.
5. **The "coin +50% in 30 days" rescue does nothing on 15m**: the 174 rescued trades are no better than the ones blocked (chance 17.1%). It came from 2h/4h, where leaders make the best trades. On 15m it is harmless, being so rare, but it is not why the gate works.
6. **Even the best 15m version is weak, by your own bar** (win rate ≥ 30%, drawdown, consistency): win rate 29%, PF without the top 2% of trades 0.79 (below 1: the profit lives in a few big trades), median coin max drawdown −53%, and after 0.05% slippage the median coin makes −3.3%. The Oct 2026 study found 4h makes more on the same coins. **Use 15m to look, 4h to trade.**
7. **Your case** (today's Top 50 gainers, 15m, last 3 months, section 7): the gate helped there too. No gate: 22/50 coins profitable, PF 1.24. Shipped gate: 27/50, PF 1.51; the 73 trades it took away averaged −2.69% (win rate 11%), most of them in the last two weeks as BTC slid under its 50-day MA. `both`: 35/50, PF 1.62. But 3 months is ~8 trades a coin, so no row clears the app's 12-trade ✓ floor on any gate, and single coins there are one or two trades.
