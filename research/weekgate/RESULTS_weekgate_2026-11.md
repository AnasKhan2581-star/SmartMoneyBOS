# The Breakout market filter on the weekly chart: BTC over its 9 MA (Nov 2026)

Generated by `research/weekgate/weekgate_lab.js report`. **Research only: detector.js, the webapp and the bot are unchanged.**

**The ask (user):** "Instead of btc daily MA rule, backtest if we change condition to above 9MA on weekly chart."

**Short answer: no gain. Keep the 50-day rule.**
- The weekly 9 MA is almost the same filter: the two disagree on 4% of 4h candles since 2018.
- Where they disagree, the 50-day rule does better on two of the three coin sets and the same on the third.
- It would not have let INIT through: at INIT's breakout BTC was −7.2% against its weekly 9 MA.
- Read the other way (the COIN over its own weekly 9 MA), it lets INIT in, and loses clearly on all three sets.

## What was tested

- **Shipped:** a breakout is bought only if BTC closes above its 50-day average, or the coin is +50% over 30 days (the "leader" exception).
- **The change:** the BTC part becomes "BTC above the 9 MA on its weekly chart". The leader exception, the chart rules and the exits stay.
- **The weekly 9 MA, two readings.** Weeks run Monday 00:00 UTC, as on Binance.
  - *Live:* what the chart shows. The MA line includes the week still forming, so BTC's price is compared with (price + the last 8 weekly closes) ÷ 9.
  - *Finished weeks only:* the last weekly close against the MA of the last 9 weekly closes. It changes once a week.
- **Set-up:** as in `research/latelead/`. Signal at a 4h close, bought at the next open; 0.1% fee a side, 0.15% slippage. Three coin sets: the 65 liqbrk 4h alert coins (Binance 4h candles to 2026-11-11), the 103-coin list, and the 103 plus 171 delisted coins (to 5 Nov 2026).
- **How to read it.** Both filters keep most trades in common. What decides is the trades a change ADDS and the trades it DROPS. Cells are: trades · winners · average per trade. The 5-slot pot is shown but it is noisy: which trades find a free slot changes it a lot.

After a rise, BTC falls under the 50-day first, and the breakouts the weekly 9 still lets through are the poor ones it adds.

## Verdict

- **Don't swap.** The weekly 9 MA is the 50-day rule made slightly slower, and slower is slightly worse here.
- **It does not solve the INIT case.** BTC was under both lines. Only the coin's own weekly MA would have opened, and that rule loses.
- **What tested better, again, is on the other side of the filter:** without the leader exception the average trade goes +1.72% → +1.90% on the alert coins, +2.22% → +2.30% on the 103 coins and +0.62% → +0.80% with the delisted ones. That is the open proposal from `research/latelead/` (10 Nov 2026), a separate question.
- **Limits.**
  - One strategy, 4h candles.
  - The 50-day length was chosen in Oct 2026 on part of this same history (32 coins), so some of its small lead is hindsight. That is a reason not to read the gap as proof, not a reason to switch.
  - The pot figures move a lot for small reasons, so the per-trade columns carry the verdict.
