# Gold accumulation: buy in tranches, stop from the average (Nov 2026)

Generated by `node research/goldacc/goldacc_lab.js report` on 2026-11-23. Research only: the app and the bot don't use it.

## The rules that were chosen

Family **DL** (dip ladder), settings T 200, D 50, N 4, stop 0.08, b 0.03, lockAt 0.1, first dip:
- **Uptrend**: the close is above its 200-day average and that average is higher than 20 days ago.
- **Accumulate**: in an uptrend, every dip buys one tranche of 25% of the slot, up to 4. A dip is a close under the 50-day average; the next dip counts once the close has been back above it.
- **Stop from the average**: the whole position is sold if price touches 8% under its average entry (filled there, or at the open if it opens lower). After a close 10% above the average, the stop rises to the average itself: a campaign that was well in profit can then lose only what a gap through the stop costs.
- **Out**: a close 3% under the 200-day average.
- Lookbacks count calendar days (gold trades on weekdays, PAXG every day), so they read the same on 4h, 1d, 1w and 1M candles. 0.1% a side, as in Compare.

## How they were chosen (spot gold, XAUUSD daily, 2003-2019 only)

Four families of tranche buying, every setting of a small grid each (468 in all). The rule, fixed before any later data was run: the best **plateau score** (the median return per drawdown of a setting and its one-step neighbours) among settings with 6+ campaigns that keep at least two-thirds of holding's yearly growth (+6.1% of +9.1%). Holding gold 2003-2019: +338%, max DD −45%, return per drawdown 0.20.

| family | what it does | best plateau score | its growth a year | max DD | campaigns |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DL | buys the dips of an uptrend | 0.25 | +6.6% | −26% | 10 |
| PY | adds on strength (Turtle pyramiding) | 0.17 | +2.2% | −13% | 41 |
| VZ | buys deep pullbacks to the 200-day in a long uptrend | 0.27 | +2.8% | −11% | 6 |
| MS | adds every few weeks while above the 10-month average | 0.24 | +6.6% | −28% | 19 |

Value zone (VZ) and pyramid (PY) keep their drawdowns small by barely holding gold (under +2.8% a year), so they miss the growth floor. The stop level made no difference to the chosen family's 2003-2019 result (8%, 12% and 16% gave the same campaigns), so the tightest was taken.

The 8% stop sold on 2021-08-09 at 1673, a candle that closed at 1731.
