## 6. Reading it

- **Liq Breakout is clearly a 4h system.** On 15m it trades 22 times a coin a year instead of 6, its median win rate falls to 28% from 41%, and slippage costs a median 9.2 points instead of 3.5. 15m beat 4h on only 33 of 102 coins.
- **BTC Cycle is the same system on both** — it is a daily system on every chart: the same trade count on 102 of 102 coins, within 5 points of net on 94. The "better / worse" split is stop fills and rounding, not an edge.
- **Turtle and Trend Follow are a coin flip between the two** (15m better on 51 / worse on 49; 44 / 54). Their lookbacks are in days, so the timeframe mostly changes when within the day an entry or exit fills.
- **The unfiltered top 10s are mostly the same few trades on both timeframes:** 7 of the 10 combos are on both lists, and 15 of the 20 rows have fewer trades than the app's floor (1–9 trades — a few long trend holds on the coins that ran: ZEC, RAY, NEAR, AR, INJ, TRX).
