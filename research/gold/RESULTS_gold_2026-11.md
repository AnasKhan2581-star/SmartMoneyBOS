# Gold, long term: five strategies tested on 50 years (Nov 2026)

Generated by `research/gold/gold_lab.js`. Gold: World Bank monthly average price (1968–2026-09, then PAXG monthly averages to 2026-10). Dollar: EUR/USD monthly average (Deutsche Mark ÷ 1.95583 before 1999). In-sample **1975–2000** (parameters chosen here only), out-of-sample **2001–2026** (judged here). A decision at a month's end earns the average-to-average return two months on (no look-ahead; about half a month slower than real life). Fees 0.1%/side, idle money earns 0%. Drawdowns are on monthly averages and understate the daily truth — see the PAXG section for real daily numbers.

MAR = CAGR ÷ max drawdown (higher is better: return per unit of the worst fall). Money = what $1 became. vs B&H = how much more (or less) money than just holding gold over the same years.

**Conclusion and the shipped rule: ALGORITHM.md, section `gold`.** G5 (clean trend, 9 months, efficiency ≥ 0.30) shipped: the only rule near the top in both halves, the best whole-period MAR, a plateau across its settings. It does not beat 12-month momentum on 2001–2026 alone, and on PAXG 2020–26 it made less than holding.

## The lag assumption

Lag 1 earns the next average-to-average month (it overlaps the decision month by half: an OPTIMISTIC bound, not achievable). Lag 2 is the one used above (conservative by half a month).
