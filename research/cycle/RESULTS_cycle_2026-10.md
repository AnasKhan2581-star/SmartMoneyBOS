## 1. What was broken

**The screen that was reported — ADAUSDT 4h, all 18,677 bars:** the engine took **60 trades**; **49** of them opened with the stop ABOVE the entry, and **49** "sold" at a price the candle never traded (above its high). $1,000 → $724,183,601.

How: a zone entry put the stop at 0.65 × the 200-week MA. When an alt is already far below its 200-week MA (ADA at 0.3× it), that stop is ABOVE the price. The next candle's low is "below the stop", so the position closed AT the stop — a price above the whole candle — booking +10% to +110% that never existed, then re-entered and did it again. Two smaller faults: the stop was checked AFTER a same-day tranche buy at the close (the low happened first), and a stop was always filled at the stop even when the candle opened below it.

## 5. 4h and 1d now agree

In March 2020 BTC daily exited on 1 March at −9%; BTC 4h held on and lost −49% in the crash. ## 6. What this still is — read before trusting it

- **A long-only cycle system.** Median max drawdown on 209 coins is still −67% (buy & hold: −98%). On the median alt it still loses (−7%), it just loses far less than holding (median B&H −85%). It is a BTC system that alts can borrow, not an alt picker.
- **Delisted coins (51):** median net −16%, median B&H −96%.
- **The half at the sell zone costs the biggest runs.** BTC 2020–21: half sold at $22.8k in Dec 2020, the rest at the Pi-Cycle top ($59.9k). ADA 2020–21: half at $0.081, rest at $1.10. It pays on the many runs that round-trip (most alts after 2021) — 2nd-half results above.
- **Every window ends in Oct 2026**, inside a BTC drawdown, which is a bad moment to mark a long-only system.
- These are backtests on candle closes with 0.1% fees; no slippage beyond a stop filled at the open when it gaps.
