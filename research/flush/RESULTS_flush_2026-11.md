# BTC flush → alt bounce: a strategy from the user's own pattern (Nov 2026)

Generated by `research/flush/flush_report.js`. **Research only: the app, detector.js and the bot are unchanged.**

**The user's pattern:** "In bad market conditions BTC suddenly drops hard (big red 15m candles), then from a POI it jumps a little, and smaller coins and recent gainers jump up more."

**Why it can be real (the trading logic behind every rule below).** In a downtrend, dip buyers keep re-entering with leverage, and their stops and liquidation prices cluster just under the obvious recent lows. A sharp BTC drop through those lows triggers them, and forced selling makes the drop overshoot in minutes: that is the big red 15m candle with climax volume. When the forced sellers are done, shorts cover and price snaps back. Alts fall harder (thinner books, more leverage) and bounce harder. In a bear market the bounce usually fails within a day, so any trade on it is short.

**The setup, fixed from that logic before any result was seen** (every grid row below changes ONE of these):
- bad market: BTC under its 50-day MA when the drop starts;
- flush: BTC falls ≥ 1 daily ATR (a full day's range) from its 2-hour high on a red 15m candle, with a 15m volume ≥ 3× the day's average;
- POI: the flush takes out the lowest low of the previous 3 days (the stops under it);
- data: the user's 103 Compare coins, full Binance 15m history, a coin counting only with ≥ $1M a day of volume; the survivorship check adds 171 delisted or collapsed coins.

## Verdict: a two-mode flush playbook that makes money

1. **The pattern is real, but buying after BTC "jumps a little" comes too late.** By the bounce trigger, BTC is already a median 2.3% off its flush low and the alts 3.6% off theirs. After the trigger they drift sideways and down, so that version loses ~0.6–1% a trade after costs, whichever coins are picked (section 2).
2. **What works is being in the order book when the flush ends, in two modes chosen by BTC's 50-day MA.** The flush rules are the ones set first (≥ 1 daily ATR down in ≤ 2h, ≥ 3× volume, the 3-day low swept); judged at the 15m close.
   - **Bad market (BTC under its 50-day MA): bid deep.** Limit buys ¾ of each coin's daily ATR under its price on the 5 liquid coins that have fallen most in the flush, 20% of the pot each, valid 2 hours. Buying at market here is a coin flip (section 4: +0.05% a trade).
   - **Good market (BTC over its 50-day MA): buy at once.** Market buys on the same 5 coins at the flush candle's close.
   - Both: take-profit +½ daily ATR (a resting limit), stop −1 daily ATR, out after 4 hours.
3. **Result:**
   - On the user's 103 coins, 2017–2026: 88 flushes with a trade, 371 trades, +1.88% a trade after 0.1% fees and 0.15% slippage, 74% winners. The pot grows ×3.87 (+16.2% a year), worst drawdown −8.5%, worst single flush −8.5%.
   - With 171 delisted or collapsed coins added (LUNA, FTT and the rest): ×4.41 (+17.9% a year), drawdown −11.8%.
   - At 0.5% slippage a side: ×2.79.
   - Year by year (103 coins): 2017 +13% · 2018 +7% · 2019 +3% · 2020 +41% · 2021 +33% · 2022 −3% · 2023 +19% · 2024 +24% · 2025 +10% · 2026 +5%.
4. **It is the flush, not the coins.** The same buys at random moments of the same market do far worse (section 7): good-market market buys −0.42% a trade (+1.79% at the flushes), bad-market deep bids +0.09% on 36 trades (+2.39% at the flushes).
5. **Honest limits:**
   - Only ~10 flushes a year, each lasting ≤ 6 hours, so the pot sits in cash > 99% of the time.
   - It needs a fast reaction (minutes after a 15m close). GitHub's scheduler starts the bot hours late, so alerts for it would need the punctual outside trigger.
   - The fill model is pessimistic (bids fill at their own price; a fill candle that also reaches the stop counts as stopped), but a real crash can be messier than 15m candles show.
   - Everything here is a backtest.

**Where the bounce actually happens (bad-market events):** BTC falls a median −6.73% into the low and is already +2.30% off it when the trigger fires. The alts fall a median −8.37% and are already +3.62% off their lows. After the trigger the alts add only +0.16% in the next hour, before costs. **The jump the user sees is real, but it happens between the low and the confirmation.** No setting of the confirmation version makes money after costs (section 4 of the lab output: every flush size, volume, POI and bounce size loses in bad markets).

## 3. "Bid the wick": resting limit buys placed DURING the flush

Armed at the close of the 15m candle where BTC's flush is first seen (same flush rules, judged at the close; no waiting for a bounce). On every coin, a limit buy m × that coin's daily ATR under its price, valid 2 hours. Take-profit +½ daily ATR, stop −1 daily ATR, out after 4 hours. Fees: a limit fill pays 0.1%, a stop or a timed exit 0.1% + 0.15% slippage; m = 0 is the control (buy at market at the arm). **Fills are pessimistic:** a bid fills at its own price, never better; if the fill candle also reaches the stop, it counts as stopped; the target can only fill from the next candle.
