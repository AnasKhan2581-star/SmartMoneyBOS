# Rotation research — can Binance's next top gainers be seen coming? (Oct 2026)

Universe: **661 Binance USDT spot coins, 192 of them delisted** (kept on purpose — a strategy that only ever sees survivors looks better than it is). Stablecoins, leveraged tokens and wrapped copies excluded. Daily candles 2019-01-01 → 2026-10-22. A coin is tradeable on a day if its median daily volume over the prior 20 days is ≥ $1M and it has ≥ 30 days of history (on a typical recent day: 184 coins). Signals use data to the close of day t; trades fill at the open of day t+1. Fees 0.1% per side plus slippage as stated.

## 1. Which signals actually come before a coin's big up-days?

For every day, all tradeable coins are ranked by the signal at the close. **Top-10 hit rate** = how many of the next day's 10 biggest gainers were already in the signal's top 10 the evening before (random picking: the 'random' row). **Top-10 next day / next 7 days** = the average return of the signal's top 10 picks, MINUS the average tradeable coin, before costs. A real edge is positive in EVERY period column, not just on average.

## 2. The rotation: hold the top 3 picks, a third of the money each

Every H days, at the open, sell what is no longer in the top 3 and buy the new top 3 (equal thirds). Costs are paid only on what actually changes hands. **Random** = the same rotation picking 3 tradeable coins at random (median of 60 runs) — the bar any signal must clear. The **BTC gate** variant holds cash whenever BTC closed below its 50-day MA the evening before.
